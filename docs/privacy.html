Privacy Policy for NeatTabs

Last updated: November 20, 2026

NeatTabs is a browser extension that organizes and groups open tabs in Google Chrome according to user-configured rules. Your privacy is a core priority: we do not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing activity to any developer-controlled or third-party servers.

1. Information Processed Locally

To organize your tabs, the extension reads the following information strictly locally inside your browser:

  • Open tab URLs and page titles: Evaluated locally against your custom domain and title rules to assign tabs into Chrome tab groups, and—only if you enable the optional "Host in Title" setting on a rule—to add a [host] label to the matching tab's title.
  • Tab group state: Existing group titles, colors, and tab membership in the current window so tabs can be placed into the right groups.

None of your tab URLs, page titles, or browsing history are ever logged, stored persistently, or transmitted off your device.

2. How Settings Are Stored

Your custom grouping rules and extension preferences (such as auto-grouping and domain fallback toggles) are saved using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with Sync enabled, Google's Chrome Sync infrastructure synchronizes these preferences across your own devices. The extension developer has no access to this data.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

NeatTabs does not use cookies, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any third-party external APIs.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your rules and preferences remain stored in your Chrome profile until you reset them in the extension's Options page or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data according to Chrome's standard storage lifecycle.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the extension's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated with a new "Last updated" date before any updated version is published to the Chrome Web Store.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at github.com/carl-christiansen/neattabs/issues or contact support@macando.se.
